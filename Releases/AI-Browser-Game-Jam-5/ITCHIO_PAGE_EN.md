- **Jam Theme Connection (TORCH):**
  *Theme:* **TORCH**  
  In *MY SHADOW QUIT: THE LAST FLAME*, the torch is both your lifeline and your primary puzzle mechanic. The protagonist wields a solitary flame whose light reveals spectral pathways, ignites arcane braziers, melts dark barriers, and casts dynamic shadows. ## 3. FULL GAME DESCRIPTION (FOR ITCH.IO BODY)

```markdown
# MY SHADOW QUIT: THE LAST FLAME

> *"When the world collapsed into ash, your shadow chose freedom. Now, only the dying ember of the Last Flame can bind you together again."*

**MY SHADOW QUIT: THE LAST FLAME** is a dark, atmospheric cooperative puzzle-platformer where you control both a desperate flamebearer and their rebellious detached shadow. Journey across 50 intricate levels spanning 5 haunting worlds, manipulating light and dark to overcome lethal hazards, ancient clockwork mechanisms, and shadowy guardians.

---

### KEY FEATURES

- **Dual-Entity Cooperative Mechanics:** Control the human protagonist and summon/command the autonomous Shadow. Switch control seamlessly or solve simultaneous synchronized puzzles.
- **The Power of the Torch:** The torch is not merely a light source—it alters the physical state of the world. Light reveals solid luminous platforms and dispels void creatures, but casts dark shadows that the Shadow entity can navigate.
- **5 Unique Worlds & 50 Handcrafted Levels:**
  - **World 1 — The Ruins of the Last Flame:** Crumbling sanctuaries and ancient stone traps introducing torch ignition and shadow mimicry.
  - **World 2 — The Inverted Forest:** Luminescent flora, thorny abysses, and dynamic light roots that grow when bathed in fire.
  - **World 3 — The Hollow Clocktower:** Massive moving gears, oscillating pendulum blades, and ticking temporal mechanisms.
  - **World 4 — The Drowned Cathedral:** Flooded crypts, lethal tides, buoyant pressure steps, and resonant cathedral bells.
  - **World 5 — The Abyss of the Last Flame:** Void abyssal bridges, echo switches, relentless shadow apparitions, and the final sacrificial altar.
- **Tactical Combat System:** Discover ancient weapons—the *Emberblade*, the *Hollow Crossbow*, and the *Abyssal Scepter*—to fend off Ash Crawlers, Clockwork Sentinels, and Drowned Guardians.
- **Atmospheric Procedural Audio:** Procedural dynamic ambient soundscapes and crackling hearth fires calibrated with dedicated audio buses.
- **Responsive Controls:** Fully optimized for keyboard & mouse on Desktop browsers as well as responsive touch controls on mobile devices.
```
